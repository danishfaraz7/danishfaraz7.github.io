About

I lead threat intelligence work for the GIS-CTI team at Tata Communications. My output goes to SecOps, threat hunting, vulnerability management and executives, and each audience gets a different product.

Before that I built the CTI function at WPP from scratch, and spent three years in the Security Operations Centre at EY. That mix means I know what a SOC needs from intelligence and what a CISO needs to hear.

I like problems where the data is messy and the stakes are real: exposed assets nobody owns, a threat actor targeting your sector, a vulnerability that matters more than its score says.

When a task repeats, I script it. Most of the tools below started as a manual workflow I was tired of doing.

What I do

Threat actor profiling

Tracking groups, their tooling and targeting, and turning it into profiles defenders can use.

Attack surface management

Finding and prioritising externally exposed assets, then getting findings to the right owner.

Dark web monitoring

Watching for leaked credentials, access sales and brand mentions, and validating what is real.

Vulnerability intelligence

Ranking CVEs by exploitation evidence and exposure, not only by severity score.

DFIR support

IOC investigation and validation that helps responders scope an incident faster.

Authorised red teaming

Scoped external assessments that show what an attacker would find first.
